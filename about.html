About

Why we exist

Totten Consulting was built on a simple observation: the plans that hold up in health care are the ones built from the perspective of the people delivering care every day — not the ones borrowed from someone else's industry.

Positioning

Totten Consulting partners with organizations across the health care landscape — health plans, community clinics, and the teams building new models of health and wellness alike — to design and develop strategies that are genuinely innovative and genuinely achievable. We bridge the gap between organizational leadership and the teams delivering care every day, building plans that make employees and the people they serve feel seen and heard, not processed, so what you get is a plan you can run, not a vision you can't.

Point of View

The spreadsheet isn't the strategy

Too many organizations deploy efficiency models designed for manufacturing, supply chains, and even corporate finance in an attempt to improve health care delivery. The results are predictable, and predictably bad. Data and AI can inform a decision — they can't replace the judgment of the teams doing the work, or the people those teams serve.

The plans that actually hold up are the ones built from the perspective of the people delivering care every day — how they work with patients, with each other, with the systems around them. Understand that, and the strategy follows. Skip it, and no model will save you. Everything we do is in service to the people on both sides of that relationship.
