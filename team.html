Team

The people behind the work

The tension at the center of this brand isn't a slogan — it's grounded in Abbie's decades in managed care and health policy, sharpened by Gene's data science lens. Innovative and grounded, because we've had to be both.

CEO & Co-Founder

Abbie Totten

Before founding Totten Consulting, Abbie spent her career inside the health care industry itself — deep, hands-on expertise in managed care, alongside health policy and organizational leadership, owning budgets, answering to boards and regulators, and living the same real-world constraints our clients navigate every day.

What travels beyond managed care is her method: ask the right questions of the people closest to the work until the larger picture comes into focus, then build the strategy from there — which is why it applies as readily to a community clinic or a wellness organization as it does to a health plan. That depth of experience is what brings most of our clients to the table in the first place.

Principal & Co-Founder

Gene Totten, M.Sc.

Gene brings an analytical dimensionality — a background in data science and analytics — that augments Abbie's managed care and health policy expertise.

The data informs every recommendation; it never overrides the judgment of the teams actually doing the work. Gene's role is to make sure every strategy is grounded in evidence as well as experience.
